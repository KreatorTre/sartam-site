SARTAM

SARTAM is a dark, gritty sci-fi saga that weaves together stories of shattered lives, untold mysteries, technological horrors, and the fragile line between victim and villain.

A mature, anime-inspired saga. The main story is SARTAM: The Novel Series on Inkspired, with eight side stories including SARTAM: The Story of Paulena Pollux.
