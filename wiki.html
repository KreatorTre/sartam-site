2. Factions & Races

Mother Nature's three mortal children Locked — sartam-power-system/mortal-races-source.txt

  • Fýsians — born only on Planet Turana; full command of all four Elemental Paths; near-zero Spirit access; slow-aging; hair/eyes match birth element; corpses emit residual elemental energy.
  • Katskev (Skevians) — machine lifeforms made from Mother Nature's essence on Planet Rremufe; compassionate; made via Birth Codes; only race capable of true resurrection.
  • Mithyans — "regular" mortals originating on Baflua; full Spirit Path; Elemental use strains them; shortest lifespans; only race that can forge spirit-energy weapons.
  • Mother Nature's Warning: "Ad alterum te alligabis, potentiam perniciem ad omnia quae tibi creavi invitabis."

3. Locations

The five planet dossiers Locked

  • Cinthyatar — sartam-planets/Cinthyatar.txt. Artificial stainless-steel sphere built by Cinthyans. One city, Cytorae City, in the western hemisphere, split by forcefields into Central (richest), North, East (middle), South (lower), West (poorest). Eastern half abandoned after pollution, now overgrown ruins. Particle Transmission via phone link; no cars. Currency Relevancy (REL); "Irrelevant" = 730-day purchase ban. 48-hour day (First Cycle / Last Cycle); 24 months, January → Yraunaj. Orb Form vs Trad Form food; nanoparticle spray instead of showers; secret Privateer Network.
  • Rijilar — Rijilar.txt. West is a post-apocalyptic wasteland after the Mericonu Raids destroyed the Frynche; barter economy; old money used as kindling. East (Itallish) largely unknown to the system. Includes Black Water City / Ipras.
  • Frigidis — Frigidis.txt. Artificial asteroid world sculpted for Frigidians; tundra and glaciers; Korochee clear clouds nightly for moonlit Water Path; Chepaa (Chief Zeero) dig rock/iron; icy-blue moonstones; near Turana. (Volumes call the Krystl/Korochee world "Ahquidis/Aquidis" — relationship to Frigidis is a gap.)
  • Rhuhun — Rhuhun.txt. Desert world over 100°F; Harsaa Desert; pyramid shrines believed to hold Anipe Mansour and Chione Salim; Camorse (camel-horse); rain causes sinkholes. (Volumes say "Planet Rhu".)
  • Zorexon IX — Zorexon_IX.txt. Former Rremufe, renamed by Zorexians; charred graveyard after the Architek purge; the only world Architeks refuse to maintain.

Baflua

  • Planet Baflua Locked seed — serene, mist-veiled origin world of Mithyans; home to creatures from every mythology; site of Beboh. Vol.7 spells "Balufa". Sources: Bafluans.txt, seriuh-myers-canon.md.

4. Timeline / key events

Deep history Locked (architeks-source.txt)

  1. Skevians help the Zorexians; Zorexians seek asylum on Rremufe, terraform aggressively, then exterminate the Katskev. Only Oke and Kitchrae survive; Kitchrae dies so Oke and Rint's data escape.
  2. Oke builds the Architeks; the Zorexians activate them to save Rremufe, imprison Oke, rename it Zorexon IX.
  3. Tech-grafted "Cinths" leave and build Cinthyatar.
  4. Zorexians try to replace Knerito; Oke's hidden protocol triggers; Architeks purge the Zorexians and burn Zorexon IX.
